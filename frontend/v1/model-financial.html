Financial Viability Model

Estimating revenue potential and operating costs to ensure solo practices remain sustainable.

Inputs

  • Lease Costs: GSA commercial rate data by county.
  • Patient Panels: 300–600 patients at $50–$100/month.
  • Breakeven: Estimated at ~$250K/year.

Methodology

The model runs 1,000 Monte Carlo simulations per county to produce a probability distribution over revenue outcomes rather than a single point estimate.
