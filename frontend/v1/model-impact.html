Monte Carlo Health Impact Simulation

1,000 simulations per census tract estimating potential health outcome improvements from clinic placement.

Design

Tract-level demographic distributions were incorporated so impact estimates vary by county. The model produces mean reductions and 90% confidence intervals for:

  • Diabetes Prevalence
  • High Blood Pressure & Cholesterol
  • Asthma & No-checkup rates
