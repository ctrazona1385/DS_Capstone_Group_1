Recommendation Logic

  • Strong Go: High-need HPSA classification + positive revenue projection.
  • Conditional: Moderate need or marginal financial viability; requires local assessment.
  • No Go: Insufficient community demand or projected negative margins.

How to use this dashboard

Users can filter by county or census tract to identify high-priority areas where a DPC clinic would have the highest impact on underserved populations while maintaining long-term financial health.
